Privacy Policy

Drive Studio · Effective August 3, 2026 · Version 1.0

Drive Studio (“we”, “the app”) helps you design Home Screen drive widgets, assign four slots, and prepare short connection sound cues for Shortcuts automations. This policy describes what data the app handles in V1.

1. Summary

  • No account. No sign-in. No social feed.
  • Drafts, preferences, and photos you add stay on your device.
  • Optional Photo Library and Camera access for your own artwork.
  • Optional Location (While In Use) for live GPS speed on speedometer widgets while the app is open.
  • StoreKit handles optional Premium purchase and restore.
  • No ads, no third-party trackers, no selling of personal data in V1.
  • Background removal runs on-device (ONNX). We do not upload your photos to our servers for cutout.

2. Data we process on your device

Widget drafts & preferences. Templates you edit, slot assignments, vehicle selections, sound cue choices, and settings (including the manual “Car connected” toggle) are stored locally on your device (app sandbox / Shared Preferences / App Group shared state when widgets are enabled).

Photos & camera images. If you grant Photo Library or Camera permission, images you choose may be used as garage art or widget layers. They remain on-device unless you export or share them yourself through the system share sheet.

Location (GPS speed). If you grant Location When In Use, the app may read approximate speed derived from GPS while Drive Studio is in the foreground, solely to drive speedometer widgets and related telemetry shown in-app. We do not build a location history server-side. Deny permission and speedometers show Unavailable — we never invent a speed.

Device sensors already exposed by iOS. Battery level/charging state and network online/offline may be read to mirror live glance data in the studio preview and App Group payload for Home Screen widgets.

3. Purchases (StoreKit)

Optional Premium is a one-time non-consumable In-App Purchase (drive_studio_premium). Apple processes payment and purchase history via StoreKit. We store only a local entitlement flag needed to unlock Premium templates and cues. We do not receive your full payment card details.

4. What we do not do (V1)

  • No advertising SDKs or App Tracking Transparency prompts.
  • No analytics / crash SDKs that phone home (none shipped in V1).
  • No account system, profiles, or cloud sync of drafts.
  • No sale of personal information to data brokers.
  • No custom CarPlay UI shell (Apple does not allow third-party dashboard replacements). Drive Studio uses Home Screen widgets + Shortcuts.

5. Background removal

“Remove BG” runs locally on supported devices using an on-device model. Photos are not sent to Drive Studio servers for background removal in V1.

6. Children

Drive Studio is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app to upload images of others without appropriate rights and consent.

7. Your choices

  • Revoke Photo, Camera, or Location access anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Clear drafts / custom vehicle images from in-app Settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove on-device data associated with it.
  • Restore or manage Premium via your Apple ID / App Store purchase history.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when features change (for example if cloud sync or diagnostics are added later). Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, in-app.
