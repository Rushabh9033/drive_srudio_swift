Drive Studio · Help & contact

Drive Studio designs Home Screen drive widgets, four slot assignments, and short connect / disconnect / reminder cues for Shortcuts. It is not a custom CarPlay dashboard shell — Apple does not allow third-party CarPlay UI replacements.

Quick answers

  • Where is my data? Drafts and photos stay on your iPhone. No account required.
  • GPS speed blank? Grant Location While In Use and wait for a fix while the app is open. Otherwise widgets show Unavailable.
  • Home Screen widgets missing? After Wave 2 (WidgetKit linked on Mac), long-press Home Screen → Add Widget → Drive Studio → Slot 1–4.
  • Premium? One-time purchase drive_studio_premium. Use Restore purchases with the same Apple ID after reinstall.
  • Remove BG? Runs on-device. Use Gallery or Camera for your own car photos — no stock OEM car library.
  • Sounds in the car? Wire Connect / Disconnect cues in Shortcuts → Automation → CarPlay or Bluetooth.
